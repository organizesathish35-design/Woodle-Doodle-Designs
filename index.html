hello — who I am & what we do

Hey! I’m Sumouli, an illustrator from India and the founder of Woodle Doodle Designs. At WDD we illustrate children’s books, create customised illustrations and invitation cards, and make art for brands like Google, Netflix and WhatsApp. We also run a learning platform for budding artists, to make learning art more affordable.
