how a piece gets made

From a small moment to a whole world.

step 1 · notice
  1. 01

    Notice

    It starts with a small moment I can’t stop thinking about. Rain on a window. A cat who’s claimed the warmest spot. A cup of chai slowly going cold. I scribble it down before it gets away.

  2. 02

    Sketch

    Loose pencil lines to find the story. Where does your eye land first? What’s quiet, what’s loud? The arch of the window frames everything, and the cat gets the front row.

  3. 03

    Colour

    Flat colours next, and this is where the mood gets decided: rainy blues and greens, warmed up by one stubborn little red cup.

  4. 04

    Light & rain

    Last come the glow, the raindrops, the steam and the tiny sparkles. These are the details that make you want to climb inside the picture and stay a while.
