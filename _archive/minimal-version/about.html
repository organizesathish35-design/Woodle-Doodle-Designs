About me

Hello! My name is Sumouli.

I’m an illustrator and digital artist from India with a degree in art, and the founder of Woodle Doodle Designs. I’ve illustrated children’s books, drawn storyboards and created art for brands: packaging design, promotions, wall murals, merchandise and more.

I also run a learning platform for budding artists, with one simple vision: to make learning art more affordable.

Love, Sumouli ✏️
